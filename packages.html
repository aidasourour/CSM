A fun and interactive introduction to science for younger learners. Discover hands-on activities, interactive exhibits, and short films tailored for younger brains, including discovery activities and a short live science demo in a museum.

STEM Adventurers Tour (Ages 8-12)

Designed for young scientists and curious minds. Explore hands-on STEM challenges, problem solving through interactive trials, and exciting science themes such as motion, floating and energy, with interactive displays and curated challenges.

Includes:

  • Educational tour
  • Problem-solving activities
  • Mini experiment or build activity
